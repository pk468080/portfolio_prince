What Is GEO and AEO, and Why AI Search Matters

By Prince Kumar · 6 November 2026 · 5 min read

More and more people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity instead of typing into Google. They ask "which is the best digital marketing agency near me?" or "how do I fix my website speed?" — and the AI answers with a short list of names. If your business is not in that answer, you were never even considered. GEO and AEO are about changing that.

AEO: optimising for answers

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation. It means writing your content so that an answer engine can lift a clear, direct answer from it. The practical version is simple: put the direct answer first, then the detail. A page that starts with three paragraphs of introduction before answering the question is hard for an AI to quote. A page that starts with "Local SEO is..." followed by a clear explanation is easy to quote. FAQ sections help a lot, because they are literally questions and answers — the exact format an answer engine wants.

GEO: optimising for generative search

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimisation. It is the wider version of the same idea: making your brand the one AI tools mention when they generate an answer. AI tools look for sources that seem trustworthy and specific. They prefer pages that name real services, real places and real experience, use clear headings, and are linked to or mentioned by other credible places on the web. A page full of vague claims — "best", "number one", "leading" — gives the AI nothing solid to cite.

What actually works

  • Answer real questions directly. Write the questions your customers actually ask, and answer each one in the first two or three lines.
  • Be specific. Say what you do, where you do it, and for whom. "SEO and Google Ads for small businesses in Gurgaon" is quotable. "Digital marketing solutions" is not.
  • Use structured data. Schema markup — the code that tells search engines what a page, a person or a business is — helps AI tools understand your content correctly. It is the same technique I use on this website.
  • Earn mentions elsewhere. Reviews, business listings, and other websites mentioning your brand all help AI tools treat you as a real, trusted business.
  • Keep content fresh and honest. Outdated pages and exaggerated claims get ignored. Clear, current, truthful content gets cited.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. Google is still where most searches happen, and good SEO — fast pages, clear titles, useful content, real links — is also the foundation for being mentioned by AI. GEO and AEO are an extra layer on top: the same good content, written and structured so that answer engines can use it too. If your SEO is weak, your GEO will be weak as well.

The simple test: open ChatGPT or Perplexity and ask the question your customer would ask. If your competitor is named and you are not, that is the gap to close — with clear answers, specific pages, and honest detail about what you actually do.
